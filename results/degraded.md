# Degraded-log tests

Frozen fold models (each outer fold's model at the window it selected in the any-width regime, fit on its training cells; M6 and M8 also on their copies) applied to degraded copies of their held-out cell. Degradation order: sample-and-hold decimation with random phase, offset + Gaussian noise, rounding to the LSB. Metrics pooled over seeds (20 where noise or phase is random, else 1); rmse_seed_min/max give the spread of per-seed RMSE; n_<REASON> are mean counts per run. Errors in SOH percentage points on answered traces. detector `time-below` is the project's window detector; `first-crossing` is the project's earlier rule, kept as a baseline (fold models refit on its clean features at the same windows). The _cal columns use GPR intervals calibrated with each fold's k95 from the clean inner fits (main detector only). Current-sensor error is not tested: there is no current channel, and a gain error maps one-to-one into dq.
