# Degraded-log tests

Frozen fold models (each outer fold's model at the window it selected in the any-width regime, fit on clean training cells) applied to degraded copies of their held-out cell. Degradation order: sample-and-hold decimation with random phase, offset + Gaussian noise, rounding to the LSB. Metrics pooled over seeds (20 where noise or phase is random, else 1); rmse_seed_min/max give the spread of per-seed RMSE; n_<REASON> are mean counts per run. Errors in SOH percentage points on answered traces. Current-sensor error is not tested: there is no current channel, and a gain error maps one-to-one into dq.
